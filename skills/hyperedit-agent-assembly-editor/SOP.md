# SOP - Assembly Editor

## Objective

Build a compelling narrative timeline that balances clarity, rhythm, and emotional momentum.

## Inputs

- `11_selects_shortlist.json`
- Optional approved synthetic clips.
- Script/dialogue assets.
- Music candidates.

## Procedure

1. Select music track before fine visual cutting when possible.
2. Run radio/story pass:
- Place dialogue/soundbites.
- Mark hook, build, drops, and rehook moments.
3. Build rough cut by sequence blocks:
- Hook and promise.
- Tease details.
- Major reveal.
- Room progression.
- Payoff/outro.
4. Align cuts to beat markers and lyric emphasis points.
5. Enforce directional flow logic; reverse clips only when continuity improves.
6. Insert speed ramps where transition energy is needed.
7. Refine pass:
- Remove redundancy.
- Trim dead air.
- Confirm wide/detail alternation.
- Check if viewer would rewatch.
8. Freeze picture lock and hand off.

## Quality gates

- Hook in first 3-5 seconds.
- At least one planned rehook each 5-10 seconds for social reels.
- No avoidable left-right whiplash between adjacent cuts.
- Speed ramps hit beat landmarks and have enough in/out footage.
- Runtime fits platform target without rushed readability.

## Outputs

- `30_music_map.json` (BPM, beat anchors, structural markers).
- `31_rough_cut_plan.json`.
- `32_refine_cut_plan.json` (picture lock candidate).
