# SOP - Audio Editing and Sound Design

## Objective

Create an immersive but controlled soundtrack where music, effects, and ambience reinforce narrative and transitions.

## Inputs

- Picture locked timeline.
- Music track and stems.
- SFX and ambience library.
- Dialogue track if present.

## Procedure

1. Confirm music structure markers (hook, build, drop, outro).
2. First pass: place required transition accents.
- Every speed ramp gets a whoosh.
3. Second pass: add scene ambience.
- Outdoor, interior, drone, water, environmental layers as needed.
4. Third pass: add selective impact moments.
- Risers, hits, shimmers, camera accents.
5. Balance levels so music remains foundation.
6. If dialogue exists, duck music with smooth keyframes and clean EQ.
7. Platform pass for compression loss:
- Raise subtle effects enough to survive upload processing.
8. Render QC and listen on speakers and phone playback.

## Quality gates

- Effects never overpower core music intent.
- Speed ramp accents are synchronized and intentional.
- Ambience layers are audible but subtle.
- Dialogue remains intelligible during dense music sections.
