# Context - Audio Editing and Sound Design

## Source patterns from tutorials

- Audio is a major retention lever; strong sound design improves memory and emotional connection.
- Test rule: if music is muted, edit should still feel engaging from SFX/ambience structure.
- Keep sound design subtle and repeatable, not maximal.
- Music is foundation; effects support, not dominate.
- Social platform compression can bury subtle effects.

## Layer model

1. Music bed (structural backbone)
2. Transition effects (whoosh, riser, impact)
3. Environmental ambience (space realism)
4. Feature accents (water, doors, sunlight shimmer, etc.)

## Level heuristics

- Maintain conservative effect levels and automate around music intensity.
- Increase certain SFX modestly for social upload survivability.
- Use high pass or band shaping where dialogue clarity is needed.

## Failure modes

- Over layered effects creating clutter.
- Mismatched effect timing versus cut/ramp.
- Reusing one ambience bed across day/night transitions without variation.
