## Hook Score Analysis

The **first 3 seconds** of a real estate video determine scroll-stop rate.
Current corpus averages **67.4/100** on hook score.

### Hook Formula (from high-scoring videos)
```
Frame 0–0.5s:  aerial establishing OR exterior wide — location context
Frame 0.5–1.5s: rapid cut to interior wow shot (pool, great room, view)
Frame 1.5–3.0s: title card OR property address overlay
```

### Anti-patterns (low hook scores)
- Opening with a slow pan of the street
- Starting with agent logo/branding (0s–1s branding = no hook)
- Drone rise that takes 3+ seconds to reveal the property

## Tension Waveform Analysis

Tension waveform measures the **emotional arc** — whether the video has peaks and valleys.
Current corpus averages **72.7/100**.

### Target Waveform Shape
```
0s–5s:   HIGH TENSION  — hook, aerial, wow shot
5s–30s:  MEDIUM        — room flow, interiors
30s–60s: MEDIUM-HIGH   — outdoor/pool reveal
60s–75s: PEAK          — signature shot, best feature
75s–90s: LOW           — details, agent card, outro
```

### How to Raise Tension Score
1. Place the **best aerial or pool shot at the 60–75s mark**, not the opening.
2. Use **music energy peaks** to align with visual peaks.
3. Add **fast-cut sequences (5–8 shots in 4s)** to create tension spikes.
4. End with a **calm logo/CTA** — let the tension fall gracefully.
