# Perfect Real Estate Video Blueprint

> Synthesized from **7,430 analyzed frames**, **48 synthesis reports**, and **14 properties**.
> Generated: 2026-02-26

## The Formula

**Duration:** 90–120 seconds for social; 2–3 minutes for YouTube/listing.
**Cut rate:** ~40 cuts/minute (1.0–2.0s avg shot duration).
**Model video:** beats sync'd, tension arc with 1 peak, 3-second hook.

---

## Act Structure

### ACT 1 — The Hook (0–8s)

| Time | Shot | Camera | Composition | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5s | Aerial establishing (property + neighborhood) | drone_flyover | landscape | vivid |
| 1.5–3.0s | Address/title card overlay | static | centered text | neutral/B&W |
| 3.0–5.0s | Exterior facade hero shot | static | symmetrical | natural |
| 5.0–8.0s | Interior WOW shot (great room / view / pool) | horizontal pan | wide | warm |

**Hook rules:**
- Aerial must appear within first 2 seconds
- Address card should hold for exactly 1.5s
- The WOW shot at 5–8s is the scroll-stop moment — use best asset

### ACT 2 — The Interior Journey (8–60s)

**Room order (canonical):**
```
Living Room → Kitchen → Dining Room → Primary Bedroom → Primary Bathroom
→ (Additional Bedrooms) → Staircase → Secondary Spaces
```

| Room | Avg Screen Time | Shot Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living Room | 8–12s | 5–8 shots | Anchor space; most time |
| Kitchen | 5–8s | 3–5 shots | Lifestyle focal point |
| Primary Bedroom | 5–8s | 3–5 shots | Aspirational comfort |
| Primary Bathroom | 4–6s | 3–4 shots | Luxury signal |
| Dining Room | 3–5s | 2–3 shots | Secondary interior |
| Staircase | 2–3s | 1–2 shots | Architectural transition |

**Interior shot grammar:**
- Open each room with a **wide establishing** (18–24mm equivalent)
- Follow with a **depth_layered mid** (foreground element in frame)
- Close each room with a **detail insert** (hardware, texture, view through window)
- Use **symmetrical framing** for hallways, doorways, and bathroom mirrors

### ACT 3 — The Outdoor Reveal (60–80s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 60–63s | Exterior patio/yard establishing | Transition from interior |
| 63–68s | Pool hero (if present) | PEAK moment of video |
| 68–72s | Pool detail / water surface | Saturated grade |
| 72–75s | Outdoor seating / lifestyle | Warm, aspirational |
| 75–80s | Aerial pullback over property | Re-establish context |

**The pool is always the emotional peak.** If the property has a pool,
it should appear at the 60–70s mark — not at the beginning.

### ACT 4 — The Close (80–90s)

| Time | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 80–85s | Aerial final wide (property in neighborhood) | Scale/context close |
| 85–88s | Agent/brokerage card | Static, B&W or neutral |
| 88–90s | Website/contact CTA | Fade to black |

## Camera Movement Rules

| Movement | Frequency | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| static (locked) | ~55% | Detail shots, bathroom, kitchen closeups |
| horizontal pan | ~25% | Room-wide reveals, exterior pans |
| drone_flyover | ~5% | Opener, mid-video context, closer |
| gimbal walk | <1% | Immersive walkthroughs only |
| other | <1% | Avoid — adds visual noise |

---

## Composition Rules

| Priority | Composition | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | wide | Room openers |
| 2 | symmetrical | Hallways, doorways, pool, bathroom |
| 3 | depth_layering | Hero room shots with foreground interest |
| 4 | rule_of_thirds | Lifestyle shots, outdoor scenes |
| 5 | panoramic | Wide exteriors, views |
| 6 | centered | Title cards, focal architectural elements |
| 7 | detail / close-up | Hardware, texture, water, light |

---

## Color Grade Rules

| Space | Grade |
|---|---|
| Aerial / exterior | vivid or saturated |
| Pool / water | saturated (teal lift) |
| Living room | warm |
| Kitchen / bathroom | neutral |
| Bedroom | natural or warm |
| Night / twilight | dark |
| Title cards | neutral or B&W |
| Default master | neutral |

---

## Music Rules

- BPM: **100–120 BPM** for fast-cut social format
- No lyrics
- Build to a peak at the **outdoor/pool reveal (60–70s)**
- Sync camera changes to **downbeats**
- Drop energy on the final title card

---

## Typography Rules

- Text overlays: **max 10% of video duration**
- Property address: first 3 seconds (1.5s hold)
- Agent/brokerage: last 5–8 seconds
- No mid-video text — breaks immersion
- Font: clean sans-serif, white on dark or dark on light (no drop shadows)

---

## QA Checklist

Before export, validate:

- [ ] Hook (0–3s) has aerial shot AND title card
- [ ] Avg shot duration ≤ 2.0s
- [ ] Living room has most screen time of any interior
- [ ] Pool/outdoor appears in Act 3 (not Act 1)
- [ ] Aerial appears at open AND close
- [ ] No music lyrics
- [ ] Music energy peaks at 60–70s
- [ ] Title cards only at start/end
- [ ] Color grade: neutral base with scene-specific lifts
- [ ] No whip pans, speed ramps, or heavy stabilizer warping
- [ ] Total duration: 90–120s for social, ≤ 3min for listing
