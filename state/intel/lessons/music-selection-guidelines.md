# Music Selection Guidelines — Learned from Pipeline Run 20260227

## Critical Rule: NO Artlist Tracks

Artlist tracks in the music library (`/Volumes/Charlie/hyperedit-studio/assets/music/`) are there as **test decoys**. They sound generic, corporate, and stock-music-ish. NEVER select tracks with "Artlist" in the filename for client deliverables.

Artlist filename pattern: `<track-name> by <artist> Artlist.mp3`

## What Got Rejected

- **VESHZA - Tulip** (129 BPM): Client called it "so cheesy, so corporatey sounding." Despite being in the 120-130 BPM target range and having good beat grid consistency, the track lacked personality and felt like generic stock music.

## What Worked

- **Aaron Hibell - Morning Light Intro Edit** (172 BPM / 86 half-time): Modern EDM by an independent artist. Clean drop structure, ambient intro that builds into high-energy sustained section. Matches the modern coastal California vibe perfectly.

## Selection Criteria for Real Estate Videos

1. **Match the property vibe, not just BPM**: A modern coastal new construction needs modern EDM, not uplifting stock music. A historic estate might need cinematic orchestral. A downtown loft might need deep house.

2. **Avoid "production music" sound**: Tracks that sound like they were written specifically for corporate videos are instant rejects. Look for tracks by real artists with genuine artistic identity.

3. **EDM structure is ideal for real estate**: intro → build → DROP → sustained high energy → close. The drop creates a natural "reveal" moment at the midpoint of the video.

4. **BPM flexibility**: Don't be rigid about 120-130 BPM. A track at 172 BPM (86 half-time) can work perfectly — the half-time feel gives smooth pacing while the full-time energy drives momentum.

5. **Source priority**:
   - Independent artists (Aaron Hibell, etc.) — most personality
   - Epidemic Sound (ES_) — better than Artlist, still production music
   - Musicbed — decent quality, cinematic focus
   - Artlist — NEVER for client work (test decoys only)

## Half-Time Beat Grid Rule

For tracks above 150 BPM, use half-time beat grid (every 2nd detected beat). This gives appropriate cut cadence (~0.7s between cuts in half-time vs ~0.35s in full-time which is too fast for real estate).
